    📱 Sync steps from your phone

    The quickest way is to log manually here each day — it takes five seconds and works on any phone. If you want your iPhone to send steps automatically:

    iPhone (Apple Health → this app)

    1. Open the Shortcuts app and create a new shortcut.
    2. Add a Find Health Samples action: type Steps, for Today, and calculate the Sum.
    3. Add a Get Contents of URL action using the endpoint details in the repo's README (method POST, JSON body with your member ID, today's date, and the step sum).
    4. In the Shortcuts Automation tab, schedule it to run every evening with "Run immediately" turned on.

    Your member ID for the shortcut: join first and it'll show here

    Android (Google Fit / Samsung Health)

    Android doesn't have a built-in Shortcuts equivalent for health data, so Android folks: glance at your step widget and log it here — it's two taps.
